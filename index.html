Can ClipKeep download any YouTube video?+

No. ClipKeep does not extract media streams or bypass YouTube protections. It helps you identify a link and routes you to supported saving options.

How do I download a video I uploaded?+

Open YouTube Studio, choose Content, open the menu beside your upload, and select Download when that option is available.

Does ClipKeep store my links?+

No. This page checks the link in your browser and does not create an account or save a link history.

What links are supported?+

Standard youtube.com, youtu.be, YouTube Shorts, and YouTube Live URLs are recognized.
